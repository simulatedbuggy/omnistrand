## The Human Blueprint: A 3 GB Codebase

The human genome is a staggering biological masterpiece—a precise sequence of 3 billion letters of DNA (A, C, T, G). If we think of it in terms of data, assigning just one byte (8 bits) to each letter, our entire genetic code translates to roughly **3 Gigabytes** of information. This compact 3 GB blueprint holds all the instructions for building and operating a human being.

Remarkably, the vast majority of this code is identical across all of humanity. It is only a fraction—roughly 300 megabytes—that differs from person to person. These genetic variations and abbreviations are what make us distinct: they dictate our eye color, our height, and the countless small traits that make each individual beautifully unique. Most of these variations are entirely benign.

But biology is fragile. 

Sometimes, a single letter slip among those 3 billion can lead to devastating consequences. A microscopic typo in this massive codebase can manifest as a severe genetic disorder. Take **Mosaic Variegated Aneuploidy (MVA)**, for example. It is an ultra-rare genetic condition affecting fewer than 50 people worldwide. There is currently no established treatment, and care today simply means managing symptoms. 

## The Economic Barrier to Healing

This brings us to one of the most heartbreaking realities of modern medicine. When a disease affects less than 0.01% of the population, it falls into the category of "ultra-rare." 

For the patients and families living with these conditions, the struggle is immeasurable. But for the traditional pharmaceutical industry, the math simply doesn't add up. Developing a new drug, taking it through rigorous clinical trials, and bringing it to market historically costs billions of dollars and takes over a decade. When the patient population is fewer than 50 people globally, drug discovery becomes strictly non-economically viable. 

The current system leaves these patients behind. They are the anomalies, the outliers, the forgotten fraction of a percent.

## Our Vision & Mission

We refuse to accept that a life-saving treatment should be determined by economies of scale. 

This project was born out of the conviction that no genetic typo should be an untreated life sentence simply because it is rare. Our vision is to flip the traditional drug discovery paradigm on its head. By leveraging advanced computational models and modern engineering, we aim to drastically reduce the cost, time, and barriers required to discover and develop treatments for ultra-rare genetic disorders.

We are building a future where:
- **N=1 is viable:** Medical research and drug discovery can be mobilized even for the rarest of conditions.
- **Data over dollars:** Computational breakthroughs make it possible to identify therapeutic targets at a fraction of the traditional cost.
- **Every letter matters:** We decode human differences not just to understand our uniqueness, but to heal our vulnerabilities.

This project is a commitment to the 0.01%. Because a 3 billion letter sequence should not be permanently derailed by a single slip, and no human being should be left behind by the economics of medicine.

## Development Setup

This project is a modern web application built with React, TypeScript, and Vite.

### Quick Start
```bash
# Install dependencies
npm install

# Start the development server
npm run dev

# Build for production
npm run build
```
